## What this entry is

This repository is the round entry for the make.fun P vs NP desk round of
Friday 27 November 2026. It holds one file, `DESK.json`, naming the `pvsnp`
campaign. The entry itself carries no proof work: Lean 4 unit files are
checked and credited on the campaign desk, and this entry is how the account
takes part in the round's places.

## How the desk work is approached

Each desk unit is a Lean 4 statement about polynomial time, written in the
campaign's definitions. A unit is closed only by a Lean file the campaign
validator accepts against the posted statement, or by a checked proof of its
negated twin. No claim about resolving P vs NP is made here: credit means a
file passed the Lean kernels against a posted statement in definitions that
are still being audited.
